Implement a separate AUX / MONITOR EQ concept.

Do NOT reuse the Master EQ logic.
Do NOT reuse the Channel EQ logic directly.

Monitor EQ goal:
feedback control and stage-monitor cleanup.

AUX EQ layout:

1. HPF
- adjustable frequency
- suggested stepped values: 80, 100, 125, 160, 200 Hz
- used to remove low-end rumble from monitor sends

2. NOTCH 1
- frequency range: 160 Hz – 1 kHz
- gain: cut only, 0 to -12 dB
- fixed narrow Q, approx Q=8
- purpose: low/mid feedback removal

3. NOTCH 2
- frequency range: 1 kHz – 8 kHz
- gain: cut only, 0 to -12 dB
- fixed narrow Q, approx Q=8
- purpose: upper-mid/high feedback removal

4. PRESENCE / HIGH
- gentle high/presence band
- suggested frequency: 4 kHz or high shelf
- gain limited, e.g. -6 to +3 dB
- purpose: intelligibility, not aggressive boosting

Important:
Monitor EQ should prioritize safety and feedback control.
It should not be a tone-shaping EQ like the channel EQ.
It should not be a master shaping EQ.

The UI should show:
HPF / NOTCH 1 / NOTCH 2 / PRESENCE

Use clear musical/monitor labels, not Yamaha internal band names.
